# Brand identity & positioning

**Channel name:** AI Made Usable (@aimadeusable). Renamed from ShippedWithAI on 2026-11-21 (DEC-017): the old name collided with @shippedwithai, near-identical small channels and "AI dropshipping" search results.

Status: positioning, vision and audience are **APPROVED** (DEC-001, 2026-11-21). Everything marked *PROPOSED* still needs approval.

## Positioning
**Discover. Build. Automate. Ship.**

The four verbs describe the viewer's journey with AI:
| Verb | Viewer outcome |
|---|---|
| Discover | Understand what's new in AI and what actually matters |
| Build | Make real things with AI, at any skill level |
| Automate | Save time with AI workflows and agents |
| Ship | Put it into the world: launch, use it at work, earn from it |

## Vision
Build a global YouTube brand that helps everyone understand, explore, use and build with artificial intelligence.

## Audience (approved scope)
Students · Beginners · Developers · Software engineers · Content creators · Entrepreneurs · Business owners ·
Working professionals · Freelancers · AI enthusiasts · Advanced AI users.

AI is always the central theme. Segment needs are in `research/audience/segments.md`.

## Brand promise (PROPOSED, part of DEC-006)
Real results with AI, shown honestly: what works, what breaks, and what it takes to ship.
No hype, and no toy demos without a takeaway.

## Voice (PROPOSED)
- Clear before clever. Explain so a beginner can follow without boring an expert.
- Honest: show failures and limits, not just wins.
- Practical: every piece ends with something the viewer can do.
- English, global audience (the creator is based in India).

## Formats (approved assumptions, DEC-002 / DEC-003)
- Shorts: daily, 45–60 s, AI-assisted narration.
- Long-form: weekly (Tuesday), the creator's own voice.
