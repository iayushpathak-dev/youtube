# Content strategy

Only **approved** strategy lives here. Proposals go to `strategy/proposals/` plus a PROPOSED entry in `status/decisions.md`.

## Approved
- Positioning, vision and audience: DEC-001 (`brand/identity.md`).
- Cadence: 1 Short daily (45–60 s), 1 long-form every Tuesday. DEC-002.
- Narration: Shorts AI-assisted, long-form in the creator's voice. DEC-003.
- AI is always the central theme.

## Pending approval
- Content pillars: DEC-010 (draft: Discover / Build / Automate / Ship).
- Segment priority (which audiences to win first): to be proposed after the research phase.
- Shorts ↔ long-form relationship (cut-downs vs standalone): to be proposed after the research phase.
- Revenue model: DEC-007.

## Principles (operating, can be refined by evidence)
- Recommendations evolve with research and real performance. No large advance batches of scripts.
- Each content item states its objective and hypothesis, and is reviewed against data after publishing.
- Shorts and long-form are analysed separately.
