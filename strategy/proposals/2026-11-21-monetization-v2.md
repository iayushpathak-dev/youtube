# Monetization strategy v2: broad AI audience (Dec 2026 – Feb 2027)

> **Status: PROPOSED (DEC-015). Not approved.** Supersedes the revenue part of `2026-11-21-3-month-revenue-plan.md` (DEC-007), which was written for a developer-only audience.
> All revenue figures here are **hypotheses**. There is no revenue yet.

## Facts that shape the plan (2026-11-21)
- YPP ad revenue: 1,000 subs + 4,000 public watch hours/12 mo or 10M Shorts views/90 d. Multiple outlets report a rise to **8,000 h / 20M Shorts views from Feb 2027** (verify in Studio → Earn). Lower tier: 500 subs + 3,000 h or 3M Shorts views. Sources: `research/market/ai-landscape.md` → Platform & monetization.
- Shorts-feed watch time does not count toward the long-form watch-hour threshold.
- Inauthentic-content policy (Jul 2026): mass-produced, repetitive AI content is not monetizable.
- Channel today: 0 subscribers, 0 videos.

## Conclusion (HYPOTHESIS)
YouTube ad revenue within 90 days is very unlikely, and the threshold may double before we qualify. Revenue in the first 3 months, if any, comes from **the creator's skills** (services) and **a small first digital product**. The channel's job in this period is trust + audience + proof of work.

## 90-day milestones (proposed, measurable)
- **Month 1:** service offer page live. Free resource (e.g. "AI tool chooser" or agent template) with an email signup. Affiliate disclosures template. KPI: first 3 inbound inquiries.
- **Month 2:** first paid kit (from the most-requested build). KPI: first sale, email list size tracked.
- **Month 3:** media kit draft, review of which segment converts. KPI: revenue by source in `analytics/channel-log.md` (actuals only).

## Requires creator approval
Service offering and pricing, any paid tool (e.g. a storefront like Gumroad/Lemon Squeezy, an email platform), affiliate programmes, sponsorship acceptance criteria.
