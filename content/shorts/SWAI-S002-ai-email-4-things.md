## Script (45–60 s, 103 words of narration ≈ 41 s spoken + on-screen pauses)
| Time | Narration | On screen |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | If your AI emails sound robotic, you skipped these four things. | Text: "ROBOTIC? 🤖" |
| 3–10 s | Here's "write an email about the delay". Generic, right? It's polite, it's long, and it sounds like nobody wrote it. That's not the AI's fault. | Screen: the vague prompt + the bland result (real, recorded) |
| 10–40 s | Now give it four things instead. Just four. Who's reading: my manager. The goal: get approval for one extra test day. The facts: paste your notes. And the tone and length: friendly, under 120 words. | Each item pops on screen as a numbered card: READER · GOAL · FACTS · TONE |
| 40–55 s | Same AI, completely different email. Shorter, clear ask, and it actually sounds like a person. Save this. Reader, goal, facts, tone. Use it for every email this week and see the difference. | Split screen before/after. Text: "Reader · Goal · Facts · Tone" |
