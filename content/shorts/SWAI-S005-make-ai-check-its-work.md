## Research
- HYPOTHESIS · Asking a model to state assumptions and flag uncertain claims makes weak spots visible. It does **not** guarantee accuracy. Say this on screen.
- Demo must be recorded live. Pick a question where the AI has to assume something (e.g. a budget plan with missing info).

## Script (45–60 s, 91 words of narration ≈ 36 s spoken + on-screen pauses)
| Time | Narration | On screen |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 s | AI never says "I'm not sure". Unless you ask like this. | Text: "IS AI GUESSING?" |
| 3–12 s | Add this to the end of any important question. Money, health, work, anything where a wrong answer actually costs you. | Screen: typing the line |
| 12–35 s | "Before answering, list the assumptions you're making. After answering, mark any claim you're not confident about, and tell me how to verify it." | The prompt on screen, large, copy-able |
| 35–55 s | Look what happens. It assumed {assumption from the real demo}, and it flagged {claim} as uncertain. It won't make AI perfect, but now you know exactly what to double-check, instead of trusting everything equally. Save this one. | Highlight the assumptions + flagged lines |
